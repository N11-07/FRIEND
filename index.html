03 / THE HUMAN SIDE OF TECHNOLOGY

More than a bot.
Built around people.

F.R.I.E.N.D. began with a simple question: what if technology made it a little easier to open up?

Informed by conversations with eight students, professionals, and older adults, the project puts approachable design, emotional expression, and choice at its heart.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A little clarity
goes a long way.

What can I try here?+

This interactive concept includes a mood check-in, a scripted companion conversation, a journal, and a paced breathing circle. The conversation uses prepared responses, not a connected AI model.

Do I need to share personal information?+

No account or real name is needed. This prototype does not send your conversation, journal, or mood to a server. Journal entries stay in this tab unless you choose to save them on this device. The hosting service may process ordinary technical visit data.

Is F.R.I.E.N.D. a replacement for therapy?+

No. F.R.I.E.N.D. is an emotional wellbeing project concept, not a clinician, diagnostic tool, or emergency service. Professional care and supportive human relationships remain important. If you feel unsafe or need urgent help, contact your local emergency service or a trusted person nearby.

What’s the vision for the full platform?+

The proposed platform brings together multilingual text and voice interaction, emotional pattern recognition, personalized support, and a reviewed safety and referral system. These are planned capabilities described in the project, rather than features active in this prototype.
